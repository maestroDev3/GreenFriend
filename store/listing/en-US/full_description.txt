Green Friend makes sure none of your houseplants dries out again.

Every plant gets its own care plan for watering, fertilizing, repotting and pruning. Green Friend reminds you on the right day, and one tap marks the care as done – right in the notification or on the home-screen widget.

CARE THAT FITS EACH PLANT
• Care profiles for more than 140 houseplants, balcony plants, herbs, vegetables and bonsai – works offline
• Winter rest: less water and no fertilizer from November to February
• Water amount from the pot size, e.g. "about 400 ml"
• Vacation mode pauses reminders and creates a care plan to share with your plant sitter

KNOW YOUR PLANT
• Identify a plant from a photo and take over its care profile*
• Health check: find out what a sick-looking plant has and how to treat it*
• Light check with your phone's light sensor: is this spot bright enough?

WEATHER FOR OUTDOOR PLANTS
• Rain counts as watering for balcony and garden plants
• Heat hint on hot days
• Frost warning in the evening before a cold night, naming the plants at risk

SEE YOUR PLANTS GROW
• Growth journal with photos and notes
• Timeline, before/after comparison and a time-lapse you can share as a GIF
• Care statistics and streaks

EVERYTHING IN ITS PLACE
• Care days in your phone calendar
• Monthly and yearly care calendar in the app
• Backup and restore of all plants, care history and photos
• English and German, light and dark theme

No account, no ads. Your plants and photos stay on your phone – only photos you identify or check are sent to plant.id, and only your weather place to Open-Meteo.

* Photo identification and the health check use the plant.id service with your own API key (entered in the settings); each check uses credits of your plant.id account.
